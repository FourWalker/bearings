Pin bar: catching your eye

When the pinned task or module is blocked, needs an approval, asks a question, has an open decision or stopped, the bar already turns the attention colour and stays that way. This adds an option to make it move, so it is hard to miss. There are two ways, and you choose one.

By theme: the bar itself moves, in the theme's own manner. By Tack: Tack stays where it is and sends a small Sub Tack to the bar to make a fuss, in its skin's own manner. The setting sits in the bar's ▾ menu and in Tack's look menu. Off is the bar as it is in the Pin bar concept: coloured, still.

Option 2 · by Tack: Sub Tacks

NEWTack never leaves its spot. When the pinned item needs you, Tack releases a Sub Tack: a small Tack that goes to the bar and makes the fuss there. Tack itself carries on with whatever it was doing (asleep, answering you, holding other notices, standing on a note), so nothing it does elsewhere is interrupted. When you deal with the task the Sub Tack simply goes; there is no trip back.

Plotter's Sub Tack: the Bird (chosen), and the other three

NEWEach is shown large and at the size it has on the bar, and plays its fuss every few seconds. Click one to put it in the demo above. Release and the pen's marks on the bar are the same for all four.

Plotter. Release: the Plotter changes to its quill and note (its own writing drawing) and sketches the bird inside the note, stroke by stroke, where the lines of script would be. It is drawn facing left, the way it will fly; it lifts off the note, grows to its size, glides quietly to the bar and turns to face right once it has landed. The Plotter goes back to what it was. No pen dot and no dotted trail: those belong to Tack's own crossing. The fuss: the bird hops on the bar and beats its wing, while the pen rules a line under the whole bar from end to end, rings the stamp, strikes rays around it, and the stamp lands. The marks stay on the paper between bursts.
Claude. Release: Tack squashes down and a small Tack pops out of its head in a puff of pixels, then runs to the bar. The fuss: it runs the whole length of the bar and back, then stomps three times on the state chip; each landing shakes the bar and flashes the chip, and it raises a "!" sign that stays up. Ivory, Mint, Night and Pin do the same in their own sprites; Emoji pops a small emoji.

Rules for both

What sets it off. Only the pinned item, and only: approval, question, open decision, blocked, stopped. Working, waiting on background work, finished and idle never move the bar.
How often. Once when the state arrives, again after 30 seconds and after 1 minute, then every 2 minutes. Each burst is under two seconds; nothing repaints in between, so it costs nothing while it rests.
How it stops. Deal with it (approve, answer, decide, unblock) and it stops at once; a Sub Tack just goes, with no trip home. Opening the task or pressing the bar's button quiets the repeats for that notice; the colour stays until it is really dealt with.
Motion setting respected. Tack's motion level applies to both options: Full as shown, Calm plays the first burst only, Still (and the system's reduced motion) keeps the colour and the Plotter ring with no movement.
Tack is never pulled away. The Sub Tack does all of it, so Tack keeps answering you, holding its notices or standing on its note. With a task open over the board, the Sub Tack stays on the bar, which is still in view.
One Sub Tack for now. There is one pin per board, so one Sub Tack. It is built so Tack can release more later, for other places it needs to be at once. For the pinned item the Sub Tack replaces Tack's banner, so the same notice is not shown twice.
