Tack holds the board's notifications

When you run several small tasks at once, the only way to know that one stopped is to keep checking the side chats. Here the board tells you: the note itself says "1 chat needs you", Tack announces it with a banner in the style of its skin, and keeps holding the notice until you deal with it. A click on the banner, or on anything Tack holds, takes you to the task that raised it. This page is the reference to build from; nothing in it is built yet.

2 · How Tack holds a notice

The bell is only the arrivalWhen a notice arrives the Plotter draws its bell and rings it, as it does today. Then the pen draws the scholar again, and it is the scholar who holds the notice. The bell does not stay.
The scholar asks for your attentionWhile something needs you he stops reading: brows up, eyes on you, and the card in his hand carries a rust stamp. Every few seconds he glances at the card and lifts it toward you twice. It plays for a minute, then holds the pose.
The banner is the announcement; Tack is the memoryA banner shows for about seven seconds, never takes the keyboard, and then goes back to Tack. Nothing is lost by ignoring it: Tack holds every notice you have not dealt with, with a count.
Every notice leads somewhereClicking it goes to the task that raised it: on the board Tack walks to the note and the task opens with the question or the result in front of you. From a chat screen it opens the board at that task.
Click Tack to see what it holdsA short list, "needs you" first. While Tack holds something, a click opens this list; with nothing held, a click opens the chat as it does now.
A notice clears itself when it is true no longerAnswer the question anywhere (the task, a side chat, the banner) and it is gone everywhere. A "finished" notice clears when you open the task or the thread. When the last one clears, the Plotter's bird flies, as it does today when a wait ends.
Quiet where it would be noiseNo banner for the task you are looking at: its question is already on screen. Several notices for one task become one. More than two at once: two banners, and "+N with Tack".
No endless animationEvery signal plays a set number of times and stops, like Tack's other clips. Still motion or reduced motion: no drawing and no gestures, only the pose, the stamp and the count.

4 · "1 chat needs you": stopped for an answer, or done?

Today a note says "Working" or "Idle", and a Goal says "running · thread idle". Idle can mean finished, waiting for your answer, or broken. These are the states that tell them apart, on the note, on the Goal and in the panel.

The strip at a note's foot

Working in board-ws4m

Unchanged.

1 chat needs you · approvalwaiting 2m

The turn halted on an approval or a question. Shown above everything else; a Feature note counts its modules ("2 chats need you in its modules").

Turn finished · not seen3m ago

It ended by itself. Stays until you open the task, then becomes "Idle".

Stopped on an error1m ago

The turn failed (provider error, limit). Not the same as finished.

Idle · 2 chats open

Unchanged: nothing waiting, nothing new.

A running Goal

Calc library v1running · mode() · 2 of 5 tasks done
Calc library v1halted, needs you: "What should range() return for an empty list?" · waiting 1m
Calc library v1paused by itself: 2 decisions wait for you
Calc library v1stalled: its thread stopped but 3 tasks are open · Resume
Calc library v1finished 5m ago · 5 of 5 tasks, evidence recorded

"running · thread idle" goes away: it becomes "halted, needs you", "paused by itself" or "stalled", each with what to do next. The same line shows on every note the Goal holds.

5 · What raises a notice, and what clears it

EventNoticeClears when
A thread on a task asks for approval or asks a questionNeeds you. Banner, sound if the app's sound is on, the bell, then the attention pose.It is answered, anywhere.
A thread on a task finishes its turnFinished. Banner, held quietly.You open the task or the thread.
A turn stops on an errorStopped. Banner, held.You open it, or the thread runs again.
A Goal halts for an answer, pauses itself, stalls or finishesNeeds you (halt, pause, stall) or Finished.You answer, resume, or open the Goal.
A task becomes blocked on the boardHeld without a banner; it shows in the panel as it does now.The block is lifted.

The app already knows all of this per thread: it is what drives the sidebar's "approval" and "input" marks and the existing desktop notifications, with their sound and snooze settings. The board only has to join it to the task each thread is on. Chats that run outside T3 (a Claude Code session in a terminal) can only show working or idle, as today.

7 · Build order

  1. The states. "N chats need you", "finished, not seen" and "stopped" on notes; the Goal lines; the two lists at the top of the panel, each row opening its task. Small, and it already ends the checking of side chats.
  2. Tack holds. The bell on arrival, the attention pose and the sign, the count, the list on click, click-through to the task.
  3. Banners per skin. The Plotter slip drawn by the second pen, the pixel speech card, Approve and Deny on approvals.
  4. With Tack in the task (the other concept): a notice lands in the task with its thread loaded in Tack and the question in place.
