Bearings

Know what your agents did, what they are doing, and what they will do next.

Technical debt used to be the thing that slowed a team down. With agents writing the code, the new cost is knowledge debt: work gets done faster than anyone can follow it. Bearings sits between you and your agents and keeps the whole picture in front of you.

What happenedEvery finished step, with the evidence it was done.
What is happeningWhich agent is on which task, live, and what it is running.
What happens nextThe plan each agent follows, and the step it is on.

What it is not

Not a ticket tracker. Not a wiki.

Nobody files updates. The board reads the agents' own work: the plan they saved, the steps they ticked, the files they changed, the checks they ran. It stays true without anyone maintaining it.

Nothing to look up. A knowledge base answers questions you think to ask. Bearings shows the state of the work before you ask, and tells you when something is waiting on you.

The board

See every chat at work, without opening one.

Each task is a note. The strip at its foot says what the chats on it are doing right now: working, waiting for you, finished and not yet looked at, or stopped. A module rolls up the tasks under it.

The side panel keeps the short list of what needs you. Nothing here is typed in by anyone; it follows the agents' threads as they run. The demo below changes on its own every few seconds.

The board assistant

Meet Tack. It lives on the board.

Tack is a small character that sleeps in the corner of your board until you call it. Ask it where things stand and it answers from the board itself, then walks to the note it is talking about.

  • Ask in plain words"What is blocked?" "What should I pick up next?" Tack finds it and shows you the task.
  • It holds your noticesWhen a chat finishes, fails or needs an answer, Tack announces it once and keeps it until you deal with it.
  • It asks before it writesTack reads and points. Creating anything on the board needs your yes first.
  • It follows you into a taskOpen a task and Tack is there too, ready for a question or to continue the work.
  • Pick its modelChoose which AI model answers on the board and inside a task.
  • Make it yoursSeven skins, three levels of motion, and it can live in a corner, in the side panel, or float.

Focus Mode

Pin the one thing you cannot miss.

Pin a task or a module and a slim bar about it stays at the top of the board, on every view. It shows the step in hand, how far the plan has come, and what the agent is doing this minute.

When the pinned work needs an approval, asks a question, hits an open decision, gets blocked or stops, the bar changes colour and stays that way until you deal with it. If you want it harder to miss, Tack sends a Sub Tack: a small helper that goes to the bar and makes a fuss, while Tack stays where it is. All of it is read from the agent's own activity, so it costs no extra tokens.

Plans

Find a step. Edit it. Watch it get ticked.

Every task carries the plan its agent follows, as plain steps grouped in phases. You can search across them, reword one, add one, or tick one yourself. Agents tick the same list when they finish a step, with a note of what was done.

Click a checkbox to tick a step, or click a step's words to reword it. The count and the step in hand update as you go, which is what the Pin bar reads.
