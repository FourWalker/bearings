Tack, living on the board

No "Ask Tack" button. Tack lives on the board itself: it sleeps in its corner of the panel, walks over to the notes it's talking about, pins new notes where they belong, and talks in speech bubbles that hold the conversation. Concept only, nothing here is built.

2 · Rules that make it feel alive, without getting in the way

It has a homeA bed in the corner of the panel. When nothing is happening, Tack walks back and goes to sleep. You always know where to find it.
It goes where the work isWhen an answer is about a note, Tack walks over and stands on it, and that note is highlighted. This uses the "show a task in the open window" channel the board already has.
The bubble is the chatA speech bubble shows Tack's latest reply, with an input for your answer. The full history is a "Tack" tab in the panel, next to "Where things stand". There's no separate chat window.
It speaks up, but rarelyIt only interrupts for things you'd want to know: a task got blocked, a Goal finished, or it needs your approval. At most one interruption every 10 minutes, never while you're typing, and none at all in quiet mode.
You can pick it upDrag Tack onto a note to ask about that note. Drop it anywhere else and it stays put. T calls it to you and Esc sends it home.
Cheap when quietTack only moves when something happens. Asleep, it's a 2 fps loop that stops after a minute. With reduced motion turned on, it jumps between spots and holds a pose instead of walking.

3 · Three ways Tack could live on the board

The interactive mock above is A. B and C are calmer options if roaming feels like too much.

A · Roamer (recommended)

Tack walks the board, and the speech bubble is the chat.
  • Feels alive: it stands next to what it's talking about
  • Keeps the board in full view
  • Needs the rules above so it doesn't get in the way

B · Host of the panel

Tack sits at the top of the panel and tells you where things stand in its own words.
  • The panel becomes a conversation that mixes status and chat
  • Calm: Tack only hops out to point at a note
  • Takes more space away from the panel's lists

C · On the header

Tack sits on the board's header next to an "Ask the board…" box.
  • Most discoverable, like a search bar with a face
  • Answers drop down as a sheet under the header
  • Less of a character: mostly a mascot

4 · Everywhere else Tack shows up

Refund flow edge cases
Task page. Tack comes along and stands on the task's header. The speech bubble opens downward and is about this task by default.
Mobile. There isn't room to walk around, so Tack lives on the bottom bar. Tapping it opens a sheet with the speech bubble at the top and the history below.
Goals screen. While a Goal runs, Tack sits by its card in the "write" pose and pins each task as it finishes. It's the same character, so it all feels like one board.
