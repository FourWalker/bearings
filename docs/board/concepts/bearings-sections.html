The board

See every chat at work, without opening one.

Each task is a note. The strip at its foot says what the chats on it are doing right now: working, waiting for you, finished and not yet looked at, or stopped. A module rolls up the tasks under it.

The side panel keeps the short list of what needs you. Nothing here is typed in by anyone; it follows the agents' threads as they run. The demo below changes on its own every few seconds.

Plans

Find a step. Edit it. Watch it get ticked.

Every task carries the plan its agent follows, as plain steps grouped in phases. You can search across them, reword one, add one, or tick one yourself. Agents tick the same list when they finish a step, with a note of what was done.

Click a checkbox to tick a step, or click a step's words to reword it. The count and the step in hand update as you go, which is what the Pin bar reads.
