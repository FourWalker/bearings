Pin bar

Pin one task or module and a slim bar about it stays under the board's header everywhere on the board: every view, the Goals screen, a master task's page, and behind an open task. It is a focus mode: the step being worked on stands in the bar and moves on as the chat ticks it, a line peeks at what the agent is doing, and the bar changes colour the moment the chat needs an approval, asks a question, has an open decision, is blocked, or stops.

Everything on it is read from the chat's own events and the board's files. The model is never asked to report anything for the bar, so it costs no tokens.

Rules

One pin per board. Pinning another task replaces it. Pin and unpin from a task's or module's header and from a note's menu; unpin with the × on the bar. Kept per board on this device.
Most urgent state wins. Approval, question, open decision, blocked, stopped, then working, waiting, finished, idle. With several chats on the item, the chip names the most urgent and counts the rest.
The button is the next move. Review (approval), Answer (question), Decide, Open blocker, Open chat, See result, or Continue when no chat is on it. It opens the task with Tack on that chat.
It stays put. No sound and no banner of its own: Tack still announces. The bar is the place you glance at, and it stays coloured until the state is dealt with.
Theme, not skin. Plotter: a paper strip, mono labels, serif title, square pips, stamp chips. Plotter Tide: the app's sans, round pips, pill chips. Other themes get the Plotter Tide layout in their own colours.
What the slot shows. A pinned task: the plan step in hand. A pinned module: the task being done under it. But when a chat works on the module itself, the slot shows the module's own step in hand, and that wins even while another chat works one of its tasks.
The step in hand always shows. It has its own slot beside the pips and stays until the chat ticks it; then the pip fills and the next step takes the slot. Nothing important is shown only for a moment.
Catching your eye is a choice. When the pinned item needs you, the bar can also move: by the theme, or by Tack. See the attention concept.
